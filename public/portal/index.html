01 / Service Booking

Book services and follow every project stage

For individuals and companies requesting Lions Entertainment services. Requests, quotations, approvals, payments, production updates and final delivery will be connected in one place.

Planned: booking.lionsent.comComing soon

Services covered

  • Photography and videography
  • Livestreaming and AV production
  • LED screens and event production
  • Graphics, printing and audio production
  • Websites and software development

Access roles

  • Clients: their own bookings and files only.
  • Administrators: full booking operations.
  • Assigned staff: only projects and functions permitted by their role.

How a booking will work

  1. The client submits a request.
  2. Lions Entertainment reviews scope, crew and equipment availability.
  3. A quotation is sent; the client accepts or requests changes.
  4. The agreed contract and deposit requirements are completed.
  5. Lions Entertainment confirms and schedules the booking.
  6. The team completes production and editing where applicable.
  7. The client reviews the work and the team handles agreed revisions.
  8. Final work is delivered and the project closes under the agreed payment terms.

Booking form details

  • Name, phone, email and optional company
  • Requested services, project name and brief
  • Event date, start/end time and location when applicable
  • Package choice or custom requirements
  • Optional budget, references and notes
  • For non-event work: a brief and delivery deadline instead of a venue

Administrator dashboard

  • Client accounts, requests, packages and quotation revisions
  • Contracts, invoices, receipts, deposits and balances
  • Crew/equipment scheduling with setup, travel and teardown conflicts
  • Milestones, deadlines, tasks, reviews and delivery permissions
  • Project messages, references and final files
  • Booking activity, income, balances and audit history

Essential booking rules

A request is not a confirmed booking. Availability must reflect resource capacity, not only whether a date already has another booking. Online payments must be confirmed server-side, manually verified payments must be audited, and file access must be restricted with defined storage limits and retention periods.

02 / Event Ticketing

Manage events from registration to entrance

For Lions Entertainment and external event organizers. Organizers will not need to purchase production services to host an event. Ticket revenue and records will remain separate from Booking invoices and service payments.

Proposed: ticketing.lionsent.comDomain to be confirmed

Private or free events

  • Invitations and guest lists
  • RSVP management
  • Seats and tables
  • Entrance check-in

Public paid events

  • Event discovery
  • Ticket sales and checkout
  • Seat selection where applicable
  • Payment and ticket validation

Public pages and attendee access

Pages will include Explore Events, Event Details, Ticket/Seat Selection, Checkout, Retrieve My Tickets, organizer registration/sign-in and Help. Event pages will show the poster, organizer, description, venue, date/time, prices and entry/refund conditions.

Free registration will bypass payment. Ticket retrieval must verify access with a secure link or verification code, not an order number alone.

Attendee journey

  1. Open an event and review its details, prices and policies.
  2. Register or select tickets.
  3. Choose a seat when applicable.
  4. Enter attendee details.
  5. Pay when required; free registrations skip payment.
  6. Receive confirmation and retrieve the secure QR ticket.
  7. Present the QR ticket at entry.

Event creation flow

  • Enter event details and poster
  • Set date/time and venue
  • Choose the seating mode
  • Configure tickets, prices and limits
  • Set sales dates and entry/refund policies
  • Submit for approval
  • Publish and share the event link

Ticket categories

Categories can include early-bird, regular, VIP, VVIP, group, table, free and invitation-only tickets.

Each category can have its own price, capacity and sales window where applicable.

Seating modes

ModePlanned behavior
General admissionSell up to a capacity limit without assigning a specific seat.
Assigned seatingThe organizer assigns individual guests to seats or tables.
Customer selectionThe buyer selects a seat; it is held temporarily during checkout to prevent double-selling.
Table bookingSell whole tables or individual places according to the event configuration.

Gate staff and validation

  • Phone-friendly QR scanner
  • Manual attendee or ticket search
  • Seat/table details and attendance count
  • Clear valid, invalid, cancelled or already-used results
  • Every check-in recorded
  • Explicit permission for reversals or re-entry
  • Staff limited to assigned events without financial access

Central administration

  • Organizer and event approvals
  • Orders, support, platform fees and refunds
  • Payment reconciliation and organizer settlements
  • Tickets issued only after verified payment confirmation
  • Safe retries without duplicate tickets
  • Defined seat-hold expiry and late-payment handling

Offline entry limitation

Disconnected scanners cannot reliably stop one ticket from being used on another disconnected device. Coordinated offline entry needs separate design and testing and is not part of the basic scanning promise.

Security and financial separation

Each organizer will see only their events and attendees. Staff will use individual role-based accounts. Ticket orders, fees, refunds and organizer settlements must remain separate from Booking accounts. Separate databases are optional, but secure access boundaries and distinct financial records are required.
